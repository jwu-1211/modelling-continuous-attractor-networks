# Provenance

`original_summary.json` transcribes and translates the source's saved shapes, noise statistics and rendering warnings. Source animations were embedded HTML/JavaScript; one exceeded the notebook embedding limit and dropped frames. Those large embedded outputs are not repackaged as verified original movies.

All files in `../figures/` and the new numerical exports come from the prepared run. The corrected Gaussian kernel, current library version and explicit seeds mean they are not exact reproductions of the erroneous learner runs. Complete reference-answer formulas are used and labelled.

`activity_snapshots.npz` stores selected custom-model activity frames, not full trajectories. `analysis_summary.json` records the comparison errors, settings and descriptive statistics. The GIFs sample frames and use a convenient viewing speed; do not infer physical timing from playback.
